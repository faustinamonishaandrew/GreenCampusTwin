# Twin
AI-Powered Green Campus Digital Twin
Twin is an AI-powered Green Campus platform that helps educational institutions monitor environmental conditions, analyze sustainability metrics, and make better decisions using data and artificial intelligence. The application combines environmental monitoring, predictive analytics, and a digital representation of the campus into a single system.

## Overview

Educational institutions often collect environmental data but lack a system that can analyze it, identify issues, and provide actionable insights. Twin was developed to address this problem by bringing together monitoring, prediction, and AI assistance in one platform.

## Features

### Dashboard

Displays environmental data including energy consumption, water usage, air quality, waste management, and carbon emissions in a single interface.

### Greenie AI

An AI assistant powered by Google Gemini that answers sustainability-related questions, explains environmental metrics, and provides recommendations for improving campus operations.

### AI Predictions

Uses historical environmental data to predict future trends such as electricity consumption, water usage, air quality, and carbon emissions.

### Anomaly Detection

Detects unusual changes in environmental data, allowing administrators to identify potential issues before they become serious.

### Sustainability Score

Calculates an overall sustainability score based on multiple environmental indicators to help track campus performance over time.

### Analytics

Provides charts and reports for comparing environmental performance across buildings and different time periods.

### Digital Twin

Includes a digital model of the campus that allows users to visualize buildings and monitor sustainability data from a central interface.
